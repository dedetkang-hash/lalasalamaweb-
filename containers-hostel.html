About this hostel

Containers Hostel takes a different approach to the classic beach hostel: rooms made from reclaimed shipping containers, arranged around a central pool and open-air common area. It's a quieter alternative to Kuta, with easy access to Senggigi's beaches and sunset views over the strait.

Like Lala Salama, the front desk can arrange transport and help you book either of our excursions.
