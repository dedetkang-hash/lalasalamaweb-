The 4-day expedition itinerary

Day 1

Lombok to Kenawa Island & Sunset Sailing

Morning pickup from your hostel in Lombok (Kuta, Senggigi, Mataram, or Bangsal). Transfer to East Lombok harbor, board the ship, snorkel around Kenawa Island, and begin the overnight sail towards Sumbawa.

Day 2

Saleh Bay (Whale Sharks) & Moyo Island

Wake up at dawn to snorkel alongside giant, gentle whale sharks in Saleh Bay. Later, anchor at Moyo Island to walk through the jungle and swim in the cascading freshwater pools of Mata Jitu waterfall.

Day 3

Komodo Island, Pink Beach & Padar Island

Guided trek on Komodo Island accompanied by national park rangers to spot wild Komodo dragons. Afterwards, relax and snorkel at the world-famous Pink Beach before hiking to the top of Padar Island for sunset.

Day 4

Manta Point, Kelor Island & Labuan Bajo

Morning drift snorkel with giant manta rays at Manta Point. Final beach swim and panoramic island viewpoint at Kelor Island before docking at Labuan Bajo harbor (Flores) around 2:00 PM.

Good to know

Included

  • 4 days and 3 nights accommodation onboard as booked
  • All meals onboard (freshly cooked breakfast, lunch, and dinner)
  • Unlimited drinking mineral water, tea, and local coffee
  • Snorkeling gear (mask and snorkel) and life jackets
  • Experienced Indonesian captain, boat crew, and English-speaking tour guide
  • Pickup transfer from your accommodation in Lombok

Not included

  • Komodo National Park entrance & ranger government conservation fees
  • Flights departing from Labuan Bajo (LBJ)
  • Accommodation in Labuan Bajo after the tour concludes
  • Beer, soft drinks, or personal snacks (available for purchase onboard)
  • Personal travel insurance
  • Crew tips (optional and appreciated)

Packing recommendations

  • Original passport (mandatory for harbor clearance)
  • Light casual clothes, swimwear & beach towel
  • Comfortable trekking shoes or sneakers for Padar Island
  • Reef-safe sunscreen & mosquito repellent
  • Power bank & dry bag for camera / phone
