Mount Rinjani Trekking

A direct climb to Rinjani's 3,726m summit. Camp on the crater rim at sunset, then climb before dawn to watch the sun rise over Lombok.

Sembalun LawangMeeting point
1–10 peopleGroup size
Book at receptionHow to book

Two ways to do the climb

Both routes start and finish in Sembalun, with pick-up at 5 AM from Senggigi, Mataram or Bangsal Port. Choose the option that fits your time and pace.

2 Days, 1 Night — USD 139 / person

Route: Sembalun → Crater rim → Summit → SembalunGroup: 1–10 people
Day 1

Sembalun to the crater rim

Breakfast in Senaru, then meet your guide and register at the park office. Hiking starts from Sembalun at 8 AM, reaching the crater rim (2,639m) around 4 PM for sunset, dinner and camping.

Day 2

Summit & descent

Wake at 2 AM and climb to the summit for sunrise. Breakfast back on the rim around 7 AM, then descend, reaching Sembalun by midday for drop-off.

3 Days, 2 Nights — USD 158 / person

Route: Sembalun → Crater rim → Summit → Segara Anak Lake → ToreanGroup: 1–10 people
Day 1

Sembalun to the crater rim

Breakfast in Senaru, hiking starts from Sembalun at 8 AM, camping on the crater rim with sunset views.

Day 2

Summit & Segara Anak Lake

Climb to the summit for sunrise, breakfast on the rim, then descend to Segara Anak Lake — relax in the hot springs and camp by the water.

Day 3

Rainforest to Torean

Sunrise breakfast at the lake, then trek through the rainforest past waterfalls to Torean village for drop-off.
