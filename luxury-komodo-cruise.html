Komodo Phinisi Cruise

The ultimate way to sail to Komodo. A handcrafted 34-meter Phinisi yacht with air-conditioned cabins, full navigation and safety equipment, and a premium level of service — Lombok to Labuan Bajo, 4 days / 3 nights.

Boat specification & facility

Boat specification

  • Length: 34 meters — Beam: 6.5 meters
  • Cruising speed: 7–10 knots
  • Fresh water capacity: 8,000 litres
  • Fuel capacity: 3,000 litres
  • Engine: Mitsubishi 6D22 & Mitsubishi 10D22
  • Generator: 2 generators, running 24 hours
  • Navigation: compass, radar, AIS, GPS, map
  • Radio: VHF radio
  • Safety: life raft, life jackets, ring buoy, fire extinguisher, first aid kit
  • Speedboat: 6-metre tender with 40 Hp Yamaha engine

Facility

  • Free wifi on the boat
  • Mineral water
  • Accommodation (live on board)
  • Full meals on board, comfortable dining area
  • Indoor area
  • AC cabins, front sun deck area
  • Top sun deck area with sun chairs
  • Bean bags
