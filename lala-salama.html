About this hostel

Lala Salama is built around a shared rooftop terrace where guests gather for sunset and live music a few nights a week. Rooms range from budget dorms to private doubles, all a short walk from Senggigi's beaches.

The on-site bar serves breakfast, coffee and cocktails, and the front desk can help arrange transport and bookings for both of our excursions.
